# Company Owner

**Working context.** The provider owner watches customer commitments and team
readiness, often while also holding an operating role. A business-level issue
may need an accountable manager, while daily route edits belong elsewhere.

**First useful answer.** Is a commitment at risk, what is the consequence, and
who is responsible for the next operational answer?

**Task under study.** Find Canyon View's access risk, assign a named manager
to verify the answer and update the field, and explain what the owner will see
on return. Do not treat assignment as a plan release.

**Design needs.** A short risk queue with exact service links, accountable
operator, last confirmed state, and unresolved customer impact. When the owner
also manages work, display the active responsibility and its authorized queue
clearly rather than merging business and field totals.

**Authority boundary.** Oversight does not permit rewriting an in-field record
or silently publishing a revised route. Financial results and billing actions
must not be implied by a completed service summary.

**Source and gap.** Owner/manager exception and schedule paths exist, but the
current Home does not expose this linked business risk as a first task. The
[prototype](../prototype/owner.html) simulates accountability; no assignment
is persisted. Role-context behavior is open in
[MG-D4](../PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md).

**Research check.** Can the owner identify the business consequence and the
right operator from normal entry without falling into route editing? Ask an
owner who also manages whether the active responsibility is clear.
