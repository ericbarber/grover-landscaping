# Company Manager

**Working context.** An office operator connects customer decisions, crew
capacity, field exceptions, and proof delivery. Several services may require
different decisions in one day.

**First useful answer.** Which exact service needs an office decision, what is
confirmed, and what must be checked before anyone else can act?

**Task under study.** Find accepted Canyon View scope, check crew fit, and
release the correct plan. Later, hold work after a field access question,
verify the answer, and review a corrected proof package before delivery.

**Design needs.** Keep accepted scope, customer impact, current plan or report
state, responsible person, and consequential action in one service context.
Require a fresh check after stale state; never reuse a prior approval for a
new version. A failed read removes protected details and release controls.

**Authority boundary.** The manager may act only within the authorized
organization and current workflow. Customer acceptance is not a published
route. A field note does not change accepted scope. Draft proof is not
customer-visible delivery.

**Source and gap.** Current service release, day-plan publish, exception, and
completion-report paths exist as separate workflows. They do not support a
single Plan 8/9 revision or Crew Lead access-question transfer. The
[release](../prototype/index.html), [office return](../prototype/exception.html),
and [proof](../prototype/proof.html) pages simulate those task moments.

**Research check.** Can the manager name the current source state and next
owner before release? Can they recover from a newer plan, an unavailable read,
and rejected evidence without claiming a customer update occurred?
