# Yard Owner

**Working context.** A household customer checks a property briefly between
other commitments. They may begin with a private yard brief and later connect
a known provider. They want to decide on care without learning dispatch terms.

**First useful answer.** Which exact proposal or visit needs me, what will my
choice change, and who takes over afterward?

**Task under study.** From normal entry, find current proposal v3 for Canyon
View, understand the one-time scope and $420 total, and decide whether to
accept or ask for a change. Later, distinguish manager-reviewed delivered
proof from a new seasonal care idea.

**Design needs.** Show property, version, scope, price, expiry if present,
acceptance consequence, and next provider owner together. Say that acceptance
requests planning; it does not book a date or charge a payment. A failed or
stale read must withhold a confident decision. Delivered proof must identify
the reviewed service without exposing private draft evidence.

**Authority boundary.** The customer controls their authorized property and
proposal response. They cannot publish a provider route, assign a crew, see
other customers, or infer that a future recommendation reopens completed work.

**Source and gap.** Owner proposal/version decisions and delivered-only portal
proof have API paths. The local reviewer currently has no owner property or
active portal grant; separate proposal and outcome fixture snapshots are
needed. The [prototype](../prototype/customer.html) and
[outcome page](../prototype/outcome.html) are simulations.

**Research check.** Can a person state the exact version, the consequence of
acceptance, and what evidence closes the visit before clicking? Test stale
version and unavailable read after the normal task.
