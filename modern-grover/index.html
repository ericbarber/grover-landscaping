CRITICAL REVIEW

Where the experience needs work.

These are expert and local-browser findings. Entry-path choices and user comprehension still need participant evidence.

P1 · CURRENT APP

Managers find a tool before they find the service.

The reviewed Company Manager journey goes through Home → Manage → Schedule → Day plans. Home leads with field job counts and shortcuts, while the office decision is deeper.

Rework: a short Today queue that opens the exact service, plan version, customer impact, and next owner.

P1 · CURRENT STATE

Home can disagree with the next screen.

The first local trace found a false Yard Owner “all clear” and a Crew Lead Home that promoted a past, read-only plan as today's route. The bounded continuity repair is now live in local review.

Delivered: Home uses the protected visit read and the loaded route date. Matched service fixtures remain open.

P1 · PUBLIC PROMISE

Marketing language runs ahead of capability.

The current public site describes connected billing and completed revenue, while invoices and payments remain gated in the delivery plan.

Rework: approve a claim-by-capability inventory before changing the homepage or signup path.

P1 · PRIOR CONCEPT

The preview stops before the real handoff.

Its actions inspect fixed sample context. They cannot show whether a decision, release, offline exception, or proof correction feels safe and clear.

Rework: build a new simulated service with actual branching across the five core roles.

TEST WITH PEOPLE

Role entry and next-owner language are unproven.

Property Manager and owner-operator entry may be too indirect, and the prior preview sometimes uses a generic “responsible team” instead of an accountable role.

Test: start tasks from the public home and ask users who acts next before showing more detail.

THE WORK AHEAD

Keep current truth aligned. Build one complete flow.

01 · PLANNING

Establish product truth.

Inventory public claims, trace the current routes and states, choose the first audience, and prepare equivalent synthetic service records.

02 · CURRENT APP · DELIVERED

Make Home match its destination.

The protected portal read and crew route date now drive Home. Historical route copy is read only across both screens.

03 · PROTOTYPE · STARTED

Connect the handoffs.

Try the Yard Owner decision Company Manager release, and Crew Lead interruption. Continue with manager exception review, proof correction, and customer outcome. Actions remain explicitly simulated.

04 · EVIDENCE

Watch people complete tasks.

Compare matched current and proposed flows. Count wrong turns, context changes, version and authority mistakes, and recovery success before a React adoption slice.
