## Session close

- What could the participant do without explanation?
- Which first answer, role boundary, or recovery was misunderstood?
- Which findings are caused by the current fixture or simulated prototype?
- Which findings need a product decision, API contract, or participant retest?
- Any stopped task or sensitive material removed from notes?

Do not write an adoption recommendation until the finding links to specific
task notes and the eligibility gate in [SESSION_PROTOCOL.md](SESSION_PROTOCOL.md).
