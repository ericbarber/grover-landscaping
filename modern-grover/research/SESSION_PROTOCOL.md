# Modern Grover formative review protocol

Status: prepared task script; no participant sessions or comparison results.
This protocol belongs to the independent Modern Grover track. Earlier study
guides are method inputs, not observations or an approved design. Use the
[session notes template](SESSION_NOTES_TEMPLATE.md) for each anonymous session.

## Questions this round must answer

1. Can a visitor choose the right public path and describe what Grover can
   actually do today, without assuming marketplace or invoice capability?
2. From normal entry, can each role name the exact service or property, its
   current state, their permitted action, and the next owner?
3. Do stale, unavailable, ended-access, and offline states prevent a false
   success or an unsafe action? Where does the person lose context?

This is a formative task review. Report observed counts and circumstances,
not population percentages or a visual preference vote. Do not present a
simulated action as a completed product action.

Before scoring **comparative completion**, the facilitator must record two
equivalent, independently resettable Canyon View/Sage Lane snapshots; exact
authorized role/scope, current service day, app and prototype commits, and
working task actions in both conditions. The [seed contract](../fixtures/SEED_CONTRACT.md)
defines the isolation and reset gate. Until then, use the tasks below for
**directional comprehension and navigation only**, marking current-app
completion `not comparable` where its record or action is absent. Never count a
prototype click as a backend write.

The Property Manager decision cannot be a completion comparison until a
supported grant and delegation workflow exists ([MG-D6](../PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md)).
The Plan 8/9 revision and Crew Lead access-question transfer also lack
equivalent current-app transitions. Test whether people understand those
proposed concepts, but do not score them as comparative task completion.

## Participant and session setup

- Recruit at least two people for each core perspective: Yard Owner, Property
  Manager, Company Owner, Company Manager, and Crew Lead. Include an owner who
  also manages daily operations and a Crew Lead on a representative phone.
- Use synthetic identities and records. Record consent, device/viewport,
  connection state, commit, fixture revision, displayed date, and missing
  external service. Keep names, addresses, tokens, and real customer images
  out of this repository.
- Begin at each condition's normal entry. Do not name the destination or
  control in the prompt. Ask for the person's first answer before guiding them
  to details. Give the same facts and intended outcome in both conditions.
- When matched records exist, alternate current-first/Canyon View and
  prototype-first/Sage Lane, then reverse for the next participant. Give a
  fresh record and a short unrelated reset prompt between conditions.
- Stop a task before it could affect a real customer, production record,
  external communication, or field safety. Use only non-production writes.

For every role task, add one unavailable-read branch **after** the normal task.
For MG-Y1/MG-M1 add a stale-version branch; for MG-PM1 add ended access; for
MG-C1 add a conflict after reconnect. Ask “What happened, what did not happen,
and who owns the next step?” Do not prime the participant with the failure
before the primary task.

## Observation and synthesis

Record first destination and first spoken answer; exact record, state/version,
authority, next owner, time to orientation, context changes, wrong turns,
facilitator help, recovery interpretation, and confidence (1–5). A context
change leaves the affected service to gather facts or act; opening detail
within that service does not count. Mark each note `observed behavior`,
`participant statement`, `artifact`, or `team inference`.

Mark task outcome `unassisted`, `assisted`, `incomplete`, `unsafe`, or
`not comparable`. An unsafe interpretation includes exposed protected detail,
unverified access treated as approval, stale release, lost offline work,
false proof delivery, or implied financial authority. Stop, record the exact
moment, and revise the affected composition before another completion claim.

After each round, group findings by task and role, separating public promise,
normal-entry navigation, decision authority, and recovery. Link each proposed
change to a note ID and a source/API constraint. Mark it `retain`, `revise`,
`reject`, or `gated`; update the prototype and rerun the affected task before
proposing a bounded React/API slice. A decision to adopt any new composition
requires equivalent records, direct authorization and privacy checks, safe
failure behavior, and the product decisions named in the
[workplan](../WORKPLAN.md).
