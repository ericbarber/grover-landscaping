# Modern Grover persona hypotheses

Status: independent task hypotheses for the September 2026 Modern Grover
planning round. These describe work to observe, not interview findings,
production roles, or access grants. The [current-app authority map](../FIXTURE_READINESS.md)
and backend remain the sources for what a signed-in person may read or change.
Earlier [persona profiles](../../design/personas/README.md) are comparison
inputs; no wording or layout from them is automatically adopted here.

## First-wave perspectives

| Perspective | First question on normal entry | Task moment to observe | Current comparison boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Yard Owner](yard-owner.md) | “What decision or visit needs me?” | Exact proposal decision and later reviewed outcome | Owner proposal and portal need separate authorized fixture snapshots. |
| [Property Manager](property-manager.md) | “Which authorized property needs an answer?” | Portfolio access guidance and next owner | Grant delegation and provider question handoff are unsupported. |
| [Company Owner](company-owner.md) | “Which customer commitment is at risk, and who owns it?” | Assign accountable operator without editing the route | A linked business-risk record is not prepared. |
| [Company Manager](company-manager.md) | “Which exact service can I safely release or deliver?” | Accepted scope, plan release, field recovery, proof review | Plan 8/9 revision and field access transfer are proposed concepts. |
| [Crew Lead](crew-lead.md) | “What is my released stop, and what is safe now?” | Work through weak coverage and conflict | Current route is historical; access question is not an office exception write. |

[Secondary perspectives](secondary-perspectives.md) records Crew Member,
Dispatcher, Billing Administrator, Support Administrator, and a signed-in
person without an active role. They protect boundary and recovery work but do
not create new prototype personas or backend permissions by themselves.

## Shared assumptions to test

- A person can hold several responsibilities. The current authorized role,
  organization, property, and assignment determine visible data; a persona
  label or prototype switcher does not.
- Every primary task begins with the exact record, source state, permitted
  action, and next owner. Loading, unavailable, stale, and ended access must
  have distinct language.
- Customers see no provider-private crew or route facts. Field staff see no
  customer price. Draft proof stays private until manager-reviewed delivery.
- Phone tasks must work with one hand and intermittent connectivity where the
  role requires it. Device-held work is labeled separately from server-synced
  work.

Use the [formative protocol](../research/SESSION_PROTOCOL.md) to replace these
assumptions with anonymous observations. Revise a profile when a participant
contradicts its task, language, or handoff; keep the evidence ID with the change.
