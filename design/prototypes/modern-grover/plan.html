Earlier prototype candidate Public and workspace concepts are validated in the browser. Participant findings and production adoption are still pending.

01 / THE INTENT

One connected experience, shaped to each role.

Grover already has public entry, customer decisions, office planning, field work, proof, and recovery. This design track makes those capabilities easier to understand as one service outcome.

NeedDecisionPlanField workReviewed proof

04 / REVIEW GATES

What the prototype must prove.

Browser acceptance

  • Direct links open all public paths and five role views.
  • Desktop, phone, and narrow-phone layouts have no horizontal overflow.
  • Each role names its next action, owner, and excluded scope.
  • Actions are visibly non-persistent; dialogs return focus.
  • Design direction is labeled separately from shipped UI.

Questions for people

  1. Does the public promise describe the current product accurately?
  2. Should Property Managers have their own first-level entry?
  3. Do the five preview tasks resemble real operating work?
  4. Which composition helps people find the right service and next owner?
