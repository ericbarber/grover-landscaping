From qualification to initial service

Each commercial decision remains explicit.

Grover separates service opportunity review, owner-authorized disclosure, site assessment, proposal approval, mobilization, and crew assignment.

  1. 1
    Establish provider qualification

    Declare service categories, territory, assessment practices, capacity, and individual credential facts.

  2. 2
    Review service opportunities

    Evaluate approximate location and owner-authorized maintenance requirements without private site or contact details.

  3. 3
    Complete a site assessment

    Confirm site conditions, dimensions, access constraints, hazards, and service feasibility.

  4. 4
    Submit a service proposal

    Document the scope of work, exclusions, cadence, price, and validity in a versioned proposal.

  5. 5
    Mobilize approved service

    Complete property onboarding, assign a crew, create the work order, and confirm the initial service window.
