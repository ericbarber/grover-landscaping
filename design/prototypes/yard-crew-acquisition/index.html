From fit to first visit

Interest is only the beginning.

Grover keeps opportunity review, owner disclosure, yard assessment, proposal, and operational assignment as separate decisions.

  1. 1
    Become ready

    Describe services, territory, assessment method, and precise trust facts.

  2. 2
    Review opportunities

    See approximate area and owner-approved care needs—not private contact or exact location.

  3. 3
    Assess the yard

    Request disclosure, clarify conditions, and move on site when photos are not enough.

  4. 4
    Agree on scope

    Author a versioned proposal. The owner may ask questions or request changes without deciding.

  5. 5
    Prepare the work

    After acceptance, assign a crew and confirm the first visit in provider operations.
