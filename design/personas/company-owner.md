# Yard-care Company Owner

**Situation:** The provider's accountable owner needs a business-level view of
team readiness and service risk, often between other operating decisions.

**First answer:** Can the company deliver today's committed work, and which
owner-level blocker needs attention?

**Primary job:** Clear organization, access, or capacity blockers and hand the
exact service decision to the Company Manager.

**UI needs:** Readiness summaries with named owners, concise risk counts that
open exact work, team and organization entry, and a visible handoff receipt.

**Boundary:** Business oversight does not imply editing a Crew Lead's in-field
record or bypassing plan versioning. Financial actions are outside the current
product contract.

**Research prompt:** Does the overview help the owner distinguish a business
blocker from work already owned by the office or field?
