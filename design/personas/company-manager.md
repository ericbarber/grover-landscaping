# Yard-care Company Manager

**Situation:** The office coordinates customer decisions, schedules, field
requests, reviewed evidence, and customer-safe delivery.

**First answer:** Which exact service needs an office decision now?

**Primary job:** Review the accepted scope, publish an exact plan, respond to a
field request with a versioned correction, and publish reviewed proof.

**UI needs:** A short priority queue, plan/version comparison, crew fit and
customer impact in one context, clear release status, recovery ownership, and
the next responsible person after action.

**Boundary:** The prototype cannot treat a preview as a published plan. The
manager sees only authorized company resources; owner-only organization actions
remain separate.

**Research prompt:** Can the manager trace a field change back to the published
plan and show who must act next without navigating multiple disconnected tools?
