# Support Administrator

**Situation:** A support specialist enters only when an owned incident cannot
be resolved in the normal service flow.

**First answer:** Which incident is assigned to me, for what purpose, and until
when may I access the minimum context?

**Primary job:** Triage an exact incident, request purpose-bound access where
needed, resolve or hand off, and leave an audit trail.

**UI needs:** Incident owner, scope, expiry, minimized activity, escalation,
and explicit access-end recovery.

**Boundary:** Support access is never a general customer or provider browser.
Privacy and erasure tools retain their separate authority and audit gates.

**Research prompt:** Can support resolve the issue from minimized context, and
when is temporary access truly necessary?
