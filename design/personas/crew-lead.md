# Crew Lead

**Situation:** A lead works from a phone in variable connectivity and needs
safe, readable instructions at the current stop.

**First answer:** Which released stop is next, and is it safe to begin?

**Primary job:** Confirm assignment, access, scope, and safety; record progress
and evidence; request an office correction when the released plan no longer fits.

**UI needs:** Current stop first, large controls, date and published-plan
confidence, offline/saved-work status, photo retry, and a clear response owner
for field requests.

**Boundary:** No customer price. A field request cannot silently edit a
published route. Device-held changes need explicit retention and reconciliation.

**Research prompt:** At the property gate with no connection, can the lead tell
what is safe to do, what is saved locally, and what must wait for the office?
