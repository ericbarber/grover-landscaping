# Property Manager

**Situation:** A customer representative is responsible for several authorized
properties and must spot exceptions without reading every visit.

**First answer:** Which property needs my decision, and what is on track?

**Primary job:** Open the exact property and service, review customer-safe
scope or proof, make a version-bound decision, and retain a receipt.

**UI needs:** A compact portfolio summary, property search, actionable exception
queue, source freshness, and a direct return to the affected service thread.
Group by property without flattening distinct authorizations into one account.

**Boundary:** No provider-private dispatch, team, job execution, or billing
tools. A property not in scope must not appear even as an illustrative count in
an authenticated implementation.

**Research prompt:** Can the manager identify the one affected property and
explain the consequence of approving the exact version?
