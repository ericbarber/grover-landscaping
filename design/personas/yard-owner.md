# Yard Owner

**Situation:** A household customer wants dependable care without learning the
provider's dispatch process. They may check Grover briefly between visits.

**First answer:** What is happening next at my property, and do I need to act?

**Primary job:** Understand an exact recommendation, make a bounded decision,
prepare access for the visit, and see reviewed proof afterward.

**UI needs:** A plain-language next step, service-day status, preparation notes,
the accepted scope and version, a delivered outcome, and a clear provider-owned
response path when something is wrong. Separate pending proof from delivered
proof; never imply that submitted field photos have already been reviewed.

**Boundary:** No crew assignment, route plan, provider-private recovery, or
other customers' property data. Concern resolution, preferences, contact, and
billing follow their existing product gates.

**Research prompt:** Can the customer tell what they need to do today, what the
provider owns, and what evidence closes a completed service?
