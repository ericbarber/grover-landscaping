# Crew Member

**Situation:** A worker receives tasks within an assigned crew and may share
the same weak-connectivity conditions as the Crew Lead.

**First answer:** Which assigned task is mine at this stop?

**Primary job:** Complete assigned work, capture permitted evidence, and review
their own saved changes or escalate a blocker to the lead.

**UI needs:** Personal task list, read-only route context, unambiguous sync
state, and a simple lead escalation path.

**Boundary:** No route publish, crew assignment, skip approval, or broad team
recovery authority. Show only their own device-held work.

**Research prompt:** Can a member distinguish their task from lead-owned route
decisions under time pressure?
