# Billing Administrator

**Situation:** A specialist may review account and completed-service context
before a later financial workflow exists.

**First answer:** Which completed visit lacks the information needed for an
authorized downstream handoff?

**Primary job:** Inspect exact account and delivered-completion context, then
send an owned gap to operations with a traceable receipt.

**UI needs:** Pending-versus-delivered distinction, exact account context,
missing-information reasons, and handoff status.

**Boundary:** This role is design-only until an explicit backend role exists.
Grover does not currently provide invoices, payments, refunds, or an accounting
ledger; the prototype must not imply those actions.

**Research prompt:** What information is actually required at completion, and
which team owns a missing or disputed item?
