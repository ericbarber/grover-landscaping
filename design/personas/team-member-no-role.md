# Team Member without an active role

**Situation:** Someone has signed in but has no current workspace role, or an
invitation and membership are not yet resolved.

**First answer:** Why is the workspace unavailable, and what can I do next?

**Primary job:** Resolve an invitation, switch to an authorized organization
when available, or contact the named access owner.

**UI needs:** A calm access explanation, invitation status, safe retry, and a
specific recovery destination without exposing protected records.

**Boundary:** Render no property, customer, company, service, or field detail
while access is unresolved. A prototype persona selector is never proof of role.

**Research prompt:** Can a person distinguish expired invitation, absent role,
and temporary service failure, then find the right help?
