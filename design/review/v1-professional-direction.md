# V1 Professional Direction Review

## Review scope

- Visual foundation
- Public homepage desktop
- Crew route mobile
- Manager schedule desktop
- Original Southwestern hero photography

## What materially improved

- The homepage now has a distinctive editorial voice, original regional imagery,
  and operational proof instead of generic product-card placeholders.
- Crew Route establishes one dominant current-work path, visible sync confidence,
  readable progress, and a stable thumb-reachable navigation model.
- Manager Schedule replaces the repeated card grid with crew lanes, time context,
  capacity signals, unassigned work, publish readiness, and a focused inspector.
- Marketing, field, and office surfaces share color, type, shape, and status
  language while using different densities appropriate to their audiences.
- The design system now documents semantic color, typography roles, spacing,
  imagery, iconography, components, and state requirements.

## V1 design risks

- The serif display voice is distinctive but must remain confined to marketing,
  greetings, customer proof, and a small number of page-level moments.
- Clay risk color needs contrast testing in every text and badge combination.
- Schedule density is appropriate at 1440px but requires a separate tablet and
  mobile interaction model rather than simple compression.
- Current icon glyphs are provisional. Production concepts need one outlined SVG
  icon family before implementation handoff.
- The generated hero image is approved-direction exploration, not automatically
  licensed customer proof or a claim about a real Grover customer.

## Decisions requested

1. Approve, revise, or reject the balanced editorial-plus-operations direction.
2. Confirm whether the homepage should retain a split hero or explore a full-bleed
   photographic alternative for V2.
3. Confirm crew lanes as the default manager Schedule view.
4. Confirm the mobile route hierarchy: daily progress → current stop → next stop.
5. Decide whether clay should remain the primary risk color or move closer to a
   more conventional red.

## Recommended V2 slice

After the direction is accepted, apply it to:

1. Crew Job Detail with checklist, photo, add-on, report, offline, and conflict
   states.
2. Manager Recovery with exception queue, lifecycle timeline, ownership, and
   activity handoff.
3. Customer completion report with before/after evidence and immutable delivery
   context.
4. Responsive homepage and manager schedule variants.
