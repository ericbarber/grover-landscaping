## Open review questions

1. Should desktop managers land on a command-center overview or return to their
   last active tool?
2. Should Schedule become a calendar-first board, a crew-lane board, or provide a
   switch between both views?
3. Does Reports need to separate customer delivery operations from business
   analytics at the top navigation level?
4. Should yard owners see bids beside service history or in a separate primary
   destination?
5. Does the V1 balance of editorial warmth and operational density feel distinct
   enough to own, or should V2 move further toward either end?
6. Should manager schedule retain crew lanes as the default desktop view, with a
   calendar view as an alternate?
7. Is clay sufficiently distinct for urgent recovery without feeling alarming in
   normal operational use?
