# Iconography

Use one outlined icon family with rounded joins and a consistent 1.75–2px optical
stroke at 24px. Icons support navigation and recognition; they do not replace
plain-language labels for primary actions or unfamiliar concepts.

## Rules

- Use filled treatment only for the active bottom-navigation destination.
- Keep destructive and recovery icons visually distinct from routine status.
- Avoid emoji and arbitrary Unicode symbols in final UI.
- Do not mix multiple third-party icon families.
- Add a text label to every mobile navigation icon.
- Give icon-only controls an accessible name and at least a 44px target.

Recommended semantic set: Home, Route, Jobs, Job, Schedule, Customers, Team,
Reports, Recovery, Search, Filter, Calendar, Map, Camera, Checklist, Sync, Alert,
Retry, Share, Download, More, Chevron, and External link.
