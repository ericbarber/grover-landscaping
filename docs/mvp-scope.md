# MVP Scope and Current Boundary

The original crew completion vertical slice is delivered and the repository has
grown into a multi-persona protected-pilot candidate. This document records the
current product boundary; [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) remains the detailed status
source.

## Current personas

- Organization owner
- Operations manager
- Crew lead
- Crew member
- Yard/property owner
- Property manager
- Support administrator
- Public prospective Yard Owners, landscaping companies, property managers, and
  crew leads

## Delivered protected-pilot foundations

- Role-aware authenticated Home and local seven-persona review
- Organization bootstrap, invitation, membership, hierarchy, and audit controls
- Crew jobs, route/day plan, checklist, photos, offline queue, amendments, and
  completion reports
- Manager route planning, dispatch, report review/delivery, recovery operations,
  customer onboarding, portfolio, team, and support views
- Customer-safe report and project-bid review links
- Private Yard Owner intake and known-provider connection through disclosure and
  assessment, plus versioned initial-service proposal APIs
- Persona-specific public landing journeys, direct signup, lead capture, and
  first-party conversion measurement

## Active completion boundary

The next MVP adoption slice is the Yard Owner/provider proposal interface. An
accepted acquisition proposal remains unactivated: it does not create a provider
customer, service property, contract, job, route, schedule, payment, or crew
assignment. Explicit activation and relationship continuity follow as separate
phases.

The wider application still requires the shared-shell, field, manager,
completion-proof, customer, and team prototype-convergence work listed in the
[adoption tracker](../project-planning/PROTOTYPE_ADOPTION.md).

## Explicitly gated or out of current pilot scope

- Curated provider marketplace, ranking, sponsorship, reviews, or guaranteed work
- Invoices, payments, taxes, deposits, refunds, accounting, and financial balances
- Full route optimization, GPS surveillance, native mobile apps, or AI diagnosis
- Homeowner self-service assistant and full multi-vendor marketplace governance
- Production claims based on unapproved customers, screenshots, or performance data
- Broad AWS platform deployment before measured demand and operating ownership

## Pilot launch gates

Repository automation does not satisfy live delivery/monitoring integration,
production dashboards and pager routing, calibrated thresholds, named staffing,
human usability/assistive-technology/physical-device review, Privacy/Security
approval, or signed go/no-go. These remain required external evidence.
